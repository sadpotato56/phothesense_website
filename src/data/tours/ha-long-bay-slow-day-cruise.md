Ha Long Bay is one of Vietnam's most famous sights, and on most days it feels like it. This cruise takes a calmer route, away from the busiest viewpoints, so you can see the bay at its own pace.

You'll paddle between limestone islets and into hidden lagoons, step inside a cave in the karst, and climb for a panorama over the water. Seafood lunch is served on deck as the boat moves through the bay, and when the day-trippers head back to shore, you stay on the water for the night.
